For Diaspora

Real Estate for the Nigerian Diaspora

You don't need to be in Lagos to invest in it. SmartEase works with diaspora clients remotely, from first enquiry to handover, up to management.

Chat on WhatsAppEmail the team

A remote-first process

WhatsApp is our primary channel for a reason: it works across time zones, doesn't rely on expensive international calls, and lets you share documents, photos and video walkthroughs in one thread. Most diaspora clients handle the entire process, from first enquiry to signing, without needing to be in Lagos in person.

The terms are the same as for any other buyer: a 17-year lease with clear title documentation, or an outright purchase with a 50% deposit and the balance spread over six months. Clear title documentation matters even more from a distance, it's the paperwork that protects a buyer who can't physically inspect every step of the process.

Once you own or lease a unit, SmartEase's Property Management team can run it for you, shortlet or long-term, so an ocean between you and Lagos doesn't mean the property sits idle.

  • WhatsApp, email and video calls
  • Clear title documentation
  • Same 50% deposit / 6-month balance terms
  • Full property management available remotely
