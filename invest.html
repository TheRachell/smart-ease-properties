Development, ownership, management, income

SmartEase develops the building, sells or leases you the unit, manages it for shortlet or long-term tenants, and pays that back to you as rental income, all under one roof.

Most real estate investment falls apart at the handover: an agent sells you a unit, and from there you're on your own to find tenants, manage maintenance, and figure out shortlet logistics. SmartEase keeps that relationship intact. The same team that developed the building manages it afterward, sourcing tenants and running shortlet operations directly. It's backed by a completed portfolio: Metrohaven, Metrovista 2.0 and Metroprime, so the specification you're investing in is the same one already standing.

  • 17-year lease, clear title documentation
  • 50% deposit, balance over 6 months
  • Shortlet or long-term management
  • Backed by four completed developments

Two income routes

Shortlet: SmartEase furnishes the unit, lists it, hosts the guests, and reports back to you. Studios and 1-bedroom units tend to see the most shortlet turnover, though any unit can be managed this way.

Long-term rental: SmartEase sources the tenant, handles the paperwork, and manages the tenancy. 2-bedroom units tend to see the strongest long-term tenant demand, well suited to small families and professionals sharing, though this route is available across all unit types.
